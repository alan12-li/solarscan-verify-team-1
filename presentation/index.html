What the two mistakes tell us

⚠️ GPT-5.5 on 1086361

  • Human: no_solar · GPT-5.5: solar, conf 0.76
  • 3 other models all said no_solar
  • This is Error A — a false "solar" would send a truck to a bare roof
  • Confidence 0.76 is above the 0.6 accept threshold — the rule would have passed it

💡 Kimi on 1086408

  • Human: uncertain · Kimi: uncertain, conf 0.45
  • Gemini, GPT-5.5 and Gemma all said no_solar with high confidence
  • Kimi's doubt maps to the human's doubt — open-weights honesty
  • This is the behavior an escalation layer wants: doubt → human review

Surprising facts

🤔 Humans hesitated — only one model agreed

  • On 1086408 the human was genuinely uncertain — and only Kimi said uncertain too
  • The other three models were confidently wrong
  • On 1086361, GPT-5.5 was confidently wrong in the costly direction

💚 Open-weights = honest about doubt

  • Kimi K3 (open) matched the human 6/6 — including the uncertain case
  • Gemma (open) agreed 5/6
  • The closed models were more confident — and more wrong
  • Kimi/Gemma are far cheaper than GPT-5.5

🧮 What one roof costs

  • 4 models × 1 roof ≈ $0.005 — half a cent for the full panel
  • Open-weights 44× cheaper than GPT-5.5 (Gemma vs GPT-5.5)
  • 100 roofs × 4 models ≈ $0.50 — less than one truck visit

🔍 Why this matters

  • Cost is not the blocker — confident wrong answers are
  • The cheapest models (Kimi, Gemma) are also the most honest about doubt — Kimi matched the human 6/6
  • So we optimize for accuracy + honest uncertainty, not token price

What failed → what works

❌ Failed

  • Bare-prompt classification: inconsistent output, no confidence
  • Confident wrong answers: GPT-5.5 called a bare roof "solar" at 0.76 — above our accept threshold
  • Costly error class is real: false "solar" on real data, not just theory

✅ Works

  • Structured output contract — 24 calls, all parseable JSON
  • Clear cases separable — models agree on obvious solar/no_solar
  • Doubt signal works — Kimi's uncertain matched the human's
  • Multi-source branch — 20/20 with public records
  • Real NYC pipeline — footprints → ortho → model → permit check

Recommendation: revise, then a limited test

What the evidence supports now

  • Not "stop" — the pipeline works end-to-end on real NYC roofs
  • Not "deploy as-is" — one model confidently produced the costly error
  • Revise the decision rule: add corroboration, don't trust a single confident call

What if we do nothing?

The costly error class (false "solar") appears in real data — verification is the mitigation, not the cost.

🔜 The 100-roof limited test

  • With Con Edison parcels: real addresses → permit join at scale
  • More labelers, more roofs, harder cases
  • Also test missing/bad inputs (abstain behavior) — untested so far

⚠️ What could go wrong

  • Permit coverage incomplete (older/unpermitted systems)
  • Imagery-date mismatch (2018 vs 2024 example)
  • 2 labelers is a small panel — production needs ≥3
  • Missing/bad inputs (abstain behavior) untested — part of the limited test

🎯 What we'd ask Con Edison

N roofs with parcel IDs · which context is shareable (footprints, permits, historical imagery) · a calibration set of genuinely ambiguous roofs
